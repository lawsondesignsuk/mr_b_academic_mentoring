Academic mentoring

Personalised 1:1 teaching, not a fixed programme.

11+ & entrance preparation

Preparing for the next step with confidence.

Entrance assessments reward clear thinking under time pressure as much as knowledge. Preparation starts from your child’s current position, strengthens the areas that need it, and builds the technique and composure to walk into the test calmly.

Preparation usually covers

  • An honest picture of where your child is now
  • Targeted work on the topics that need it
  • Practice with assessment-style questions
  • Timing, technique and exam-day composure
